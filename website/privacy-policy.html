Overview

SpinoCare ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SpinoCare web application and mobile platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Services.

By accessing or using SpinoCare, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Services.

SpinoCare is an AI-assisted diagnostic tool intended to support, not replace, professional medical judgment. Always consult a licensed physician or radiologist for clinical decisions.

Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve our Services:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and selected role (Patient, Doctor, Radiologist, Researcher).
  • Medical Images: MRI scans and radiological images you upload for AI-assisted analysis.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, session duration, and interaction logs.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, and device identifiers.
  • Communications: Messages sent through support channels, feedback forms, or in-app chat.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your SpinoCare account and provide personalized services.
  • To process and analyze MRI images using our AI diagnostic engine.
  • To send account verification emails, OTP codes, and security alerts.
  • To improve our AI models, product features, and user experience (using anonymized, aggregated data only).
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity.

MRI Image Handling

Your MRI images and radiological data are treated as sensitive health information and handled with the utmost care:

  • Images are transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.
  • Images are processed in-memory for AI analysis and are not permanently stored on our servers beyond your session unless you explicitly save a report.
  • Saved reports are stored in an access-controlled, encrypted database linked only to your account.
  • Your images are never sold, licensed, or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • De-identified images may be used for model improvement only with your explicit opt-in consent.

Our data handling practices are aligned with HIPAA guidelines for the protection of Protected Health Information (PHI).

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist in operating our platform (e.g., cloud hosting, email delivery) under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred with prior notice.
  • With Your Consent: Any other sharing will occur only with your explicit written consent.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Passwords are hashed using a strong one-way algorithm and never stored in plain text.
  • Access to production systems and databases is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • We conduct regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Request a machine-readable export of your data.
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously given consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@spinocare.app. We will respond within 30 days.

Cookies & Local Storage

SpinoCare uses browser storage mechanisms to enhance your experience:

  • Authentication Tokens: Stored in localStorage to keep you signed in securely.
  • Session Preferences: UI settings and preferences stored locally on your device.
  • Analytics Cookies: We may use anonymized analytics to understand usage patterns. No personally identifiable information is included.

You can clear local storage at any time through your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

SpinoCare is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify you via email or a prominent in-app notice. Your continued use of SpinoCare after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, our team is here to help.
