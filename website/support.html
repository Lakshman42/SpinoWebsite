Frequently Asked Questions

How do I analyze an image?

Simple tap on the plus button on the main screen to take a photo or select an image from your gallery. The AI will automatically analyze it for medical conditions.

Is my data private?

Yes, all your medical data is encrypted and stored securely. We follow strict HIPAA guidelines to protect your privacy.

Can I share my results?

Yes, you can easily export your analysis reports as PDFs to share with your healthcare provider.

Can I use this offline?

Yes, the app can work in offline mode using a local AI model. However, online mode provides more accurate results.

How accurate are the results?

Our AI model is trained on thousands of medical images for high accuracy. However, always consult with a professional for diagnosis.
